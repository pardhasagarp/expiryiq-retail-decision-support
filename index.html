Interpretation boundary

These outputs demonstrate functional logic using synthetic data. They do not establish actual food-waste reduction, staff time savings, willingness to pay, commercial retention or legal compliance in a live store. Those outcomes require an approved retailer pilot.

Rules implemented in this prototype

1
Use-by hard stop

If a use-by batch is past date, recommendation becomes quarantine/remove from sale; no markdown advice is shown.

2
At-risk quantity

max(0, batch stock - average daily sales × days remaining). This is a simple transparent planning proxy.

3
Markdown prompt

Shown where stock is near expiry and at-risk quantity is positive. The retailer retains final pricing authority.

4
Expiry-aware reorder

Near-expiry stock is discounted from the usable-stock proxy before the next-order suggestion is calculated.
