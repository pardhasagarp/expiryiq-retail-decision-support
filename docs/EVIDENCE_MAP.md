# ExpiryIQ Evidence Map

This repository separates the working artefact from the evidence used to justify and test it.

| Repository item | Purpose | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| `index.html` | Functional ExpiryIQ proof-of-concept | Prototype using synthetic demonstration data |
| `data/ExpiryIQ_Synthetic_Sample_Data.csv` | Representative batches, expiry dates, quantities and product attributes used to test prototype logic | Synthetic test data |
| `models/ExpiryIQ_Final_Financial_and_Evidence_Model.xlsx` | Market evidence, competitor benchmark, sales funnel, 12-month cash flow, three-year model, sensitivity, KPI gates and risk register | Mix of published evidence, derived calculations and clearly labelled planning assumptions |

## Evidence hierarchy used in the project

1. Official government, regulator and standards sources for regulation, safety and official statistics.
2. Recognised sector evidence for UK convenience-market structure.
3. Peer-reviewed academic research for operational mechanisms, adoption and business-model interpretation.
4. Official vendor sources for current features and pricing only.
5. Project assumptions and synthetic scenarios for planning and functional testing.

Published sector size, competitor activity and technology adoption are **not** treated as proof that retailers will buy ExpiryIQ. Paid continuation, real data quality, staff burden and store-level value remain controlled-pilot questions.

## Academic evidence boundary

The final assessed business plan, ethics documentation and detailed research log are intentionally excluded from the public repository. They are retained in the private university submission/evidence pack.
